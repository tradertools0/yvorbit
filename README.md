# YV Orbit

### Algorithmic Trading · Expert Advisor Development · Strategy Research

**YV Orbit** is an independent algorithmic trading development and research initiative focused on designing, testing, and improving systematic trading strategies.

The goal is to turn trading ideas into structured, testable systems through rule-based strategy development, technical implementation, backtesting, and continuous research.

Our work focuses on the development process behind automated trading systems — from strategy logic and indicator implementation to Expert Advisors, historical testing, and technical validation.

---

## What We Do

### 1. Expert Advisor Development

Designing and developing automated trading systems for MetaTrader platforms.

- Rule-based trade entry and exit logic
- Automated trade management
- Stop-loss, take-profit, and trailing-stop logic
- Configurable strategy parameters
- Technical testing and debugging

### 2. Trading Strategy Research

Exploring systematic approaches to market behavior and trading opportunities.

- Trend-following strategies
- Breakout and reversal concepts
- Mean-reversion systems
- Price-action-based trading rules
- Multi-timeframe strategy research

Strategies are evaluated through defined rules and historical testing rather than assumptions about future performance.

### 3. Indicator Development

Developing and implementing technical indicators and trading signals.

- Indicator logic and calculations
- Signal generation and validation
- Pine Script research and implementation
- MQL5 indicator and strategy integration

### 4. Backtesting and Technical Validation

Testing strategy behavior under historical market conditions to understand how a system operates.

Research may include:

- Entry and exit validation
- Stop-loss and take-profit behavior
- Trade frequency and holding time
- Drawdown and risk metrics
- Timeframe and parameter comparisons
- Execution logic and edge-case testing

Backtest results are historical observations, not guarantees of future performance.

## Development Approach

Our development process emphasizes:

1. **Define** — Establish clear strategy rules and trading conditions.
2. **Implement** — Translate the rules into code.
3. **Validate** — Check calculations, signals, and trade-management logic.
4. **Backtest** — Evaluate behavior using historical market data.
5. **Improve** — Review findings, feedback, and potential technical refinements.

The priority is to understand whether the implementation behaves as intended before drawing conclusions from performance statistics.
