01 / ABOUT

Built around process, not promises.

Markets change. Strategies behave differently across instruments, sessions, volatility regimes and execution environments. YV Orbit approaches trading technology as an iterative engineering process: define the idea, encode the rules, test the behaviour, inspect the limitations, and refine only when evidence supports it.

Our work is research and development. Historical or demo results are observations—not guarantees of future performance.

04 / WORKFLOW

A repeatable research loop.

A result is useful only when the rules, assumptions and testing conditions are understood. The workflow separates strategy ideas from implementation issues and observed performance.

STEP 01

Define

Document the hypothesis, instrument, timeframe, entry/exit rules and risk constraints.

STEP 02

Build

Implement the logic with explicit conditions, safeguards and traceable settings.

STEP 03

Test

Run historical or demo evaluation and record assumptions, costs and observed behaviour.

STEP 04

Review

Inspect drawdown, execution, failure modes and limitations before deciding the next iteration.

05 / PRINCIPLES

Evidence is part of the design.

We aim to communicate what was tested, how it was tested and where uncertainty remains. Strong branding should never replace sound research or honest reporting.

Transparent evaluation

Separate backtests, demo observations and live trading. Avoid presenting one as proof of another.

Risk-aware engineering

Consider spread, slippage, contract specifications, position sizing, drawdown and market regime.

Iterative improvement

Use failures and weak outcomes to investigate assumptions—not to hide inconvenient evidence.

Platform-aware logic

Recognize that symbol specifications and execution behaviour can differ between brokers and environments.

Coverage areas: Forex and CFDs, indices, metals such as gold, and selected digital-asset instruments—subject to platform availability and instrument specifications.
