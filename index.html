What is checked, and what is missing

Where a report shows gross profit and gross loss, the site recalculates net result and profit factor. 6 entries carry a data note: the image does not add up, contradicts itself, or leaves a detail unconfirmed.

When a report does not show a value, such as spread, commission or test dates, the record says so instead of filling in a default.

What a backtest cannot tell you

A backtest shows how fixed rules behaved on past prices under one broker's data and settings. Live trading adds slippage, changing spreads, requotes and outages. Past results do not predict future ones. A one-day or one-week forward test is too short to judge an EA.

How each EA is built

A result only means something when the rules and test conditions are known. The same four steps apply to every project on the record.

Define

Write down the idea, market, timeframe, entry and exit rules and risk limits before any code.

Build and audit

Code the rules with explicit safeguards, then audit the code and indicators before testing.

Test

Backtest across timeframes and brokers, then forward test on a live chart.

Publish and review

Post the result, good or bad, and decide the next change from the evidence.
