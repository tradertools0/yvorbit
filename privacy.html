Privacy

Privacy notice

This site is a set of static pages. It has no user accounts, no database and no analytics or advertising trackers.

The contact form

The form on the home page does not send anything to a YV Orbit server. When you press Send message, the site builds a message from what you typed (your name, your optional email or WhatsApp number, the topic and your message) and opens it in WhatsApp, or in your email app if WhatsApp is not set up. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app.

Once you send it, the message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) or your email provider under their own privacy policies, and it reaches YV Orbit like any other WhatsApp message or email.

What the information is used for

Messages are used only to reply to you about what you asked: EA feedback, tool access requests, testing work or collaboration. They are not sold, shared for marketing or added to a mailing list.

Cookies and tracking

The site does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking scripts. It does not store anything in your browser.

Services the site relies on

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers receive your IP address and basic request details when pages load, and may keep them in short-term logs.
  • Fonts. Text fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page opens.
  • Links to other sites. Links to MQL5, TradingView, social networks, WhatsApp and GitHub take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Your messages and your choices

You can ask for a copy of a message you sent, or ask for it to be deleted, by contacting YV Orbit through the same channel you used. You can also delete the conversation on your side in WhatsApp or your email app at any time.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything new, such as through a form that submits to a server or an analytics tool, this notice will be updated first.
